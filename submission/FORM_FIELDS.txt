5. SOLUTION DESCRIPTION (964 CHARACTERS; LIMIT 1,024)
RetailIQ connects sensing, execution and empowerment in CPG retail. Field reps capture shelf evidence, confirm inventory gaps, prepare source-linked buyer conversations and save records for manager review. Gemini supports audits, rehearsal and discovery; code computes scores and stock-aware quantities using demand, inbound stock, case sizes and shelf life. Own-brand compliance is separate from competitor benchmarking. Buyers retain order approval. Managers inspect evidence; shoppers compare catalogue options by need, price and nutrition.
