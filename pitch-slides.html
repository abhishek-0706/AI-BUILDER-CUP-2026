RetailIQOpportunities
Difference · problem solving · USP

Close the gap between detection and action.

How the idea differs

A complete visit,
not an isolated score.

The same workflow carries shelf evidence, inventory context, a prepared conversation and a saved manager handoff.

Design comparison with isolated vision, analytics or chat workflows; no exhaustive competitor benchmark is claimed.

How it solves the problem

Verify the stock state
before buying more.

Zero facings plus 64 recorded units triggers a locate-and-reconcile task. Demand, inbound stock, cases and shelf life constrain a justified proposed order.

The 64-unit scenario is synthetic. System records require a physical check.

USP of the proposed solution

Evidence + retail logic
+ human authority.

Own-brand and competitor questions stay separate. Unseen evidence stays unknown. Source-linked explanations and a saved record let the rep and manager inspect the reasoning.

Research rationale: a 55-retailer study reports out-of-stocks around 8% and links performance to aligned sensing, execution and empowerment. We apply that finding as a design principle, not as proof of RetailIQ impact.
Source: Corsten, Gruen & LaBrecque, IJRDM, 28 May 2026. Public abstract; fieldwork dates are not disclosed.

RetailIQProcess flow diagram or Use-case diagram
One visit · trace the decision

Observe → verify → act → review.

01 · SENSE

Capture shelf

Photo or selected video frames; preserve the visit context.

02 · QUALIFY

Assess visibility

PASS, FAIL or NOT VISIBLE; human confirms counts.

03 · VERIFY

Check stock

Compare shelf evidence with system and inbound quantities.

04 · EXECUTE

Choose action

Locate/reconcile stock, or size justified replenishment.

↓
08 · REVIEW

Manager handoff

Inspect the record and follow up on the unresolved action.

07 · RECORD

Save the visit

Audit, proposed action and debrief; local or configured cloud.

06 · HUMAN CONTROL

Review the draft

Rep and buyer validate counts, terms and commercial commitment.

05 · EMPOWER

Prepare the dialogue

Inspect source-linked facts; rehearse the buyer conversation.

Decision branch: empty shelf + positive recorded stock → locate and reconcile first. Verified replenishment need → demand minus usable stock and inbound, constrained by cases and shelf life.
