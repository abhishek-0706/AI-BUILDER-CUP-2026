RetailIQ connects sensing, execution and empowerment in CPG retail. A field rep captures shelf evidence, confirms gaps against inventory, prepares a source-linked buyer conversation and saves a record for manager review. Gemini supports shelf audits, rehearsal and product discovery; code computes scores and stock-aware quantities using demand, inbound stock, case sizes and shelf life. Own-brand compliance is separate from competitor benchmarking. The buyer retains order approval. Managers review visit evidence; shoppers discover catalogue options by need, price and nutrition. The implemented Google Cloud stack uses Cloud Run, Vertex AI, BigQuery/BQML, Firestore and private Cloud Storage. A labelled no-key scenario and optional unbilled AI Studio mode support free review. Commercial data is synthetic. Verification reports distinguish local regressions from historical cloud checks. Time savings and business uplift remain pilot hypotheses.
