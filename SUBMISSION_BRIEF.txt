RetailIQ turns shelf evidence into accountable CPG retail actions. Field reps audit shelf photos/video with Gemini, confirm stock gaps, prepare source-linked buyer pitches and save structured visit records. Managers review workspace-scoped Firestore records; shoppers compare catalogue options by need, price and nutrition. Firebase Hosting routes the same-origin application and APIs to Cloud Run. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite uses a separate unbilled AI project with no paid fallback. Retail code sizes proposed orders using demand, stock, inbound, case sizes and shelf life. Buyers retain approval; orders remain drafts. Own-brand compliance is separate from competitor benchmarking. Catalogue, customer figures and forecasts are explicitly synthetic. BigQuery/Vertex are optional reference implementations, not the live data/model path. Live Gemini and Firestore checks passed; sales uplift, cost and time savings remain pilot hypotheses.
