# Protiva Portfolio and Commercial Context

All content below is synthetic and written for a hackathon demo. The brand name is held as the
placeholder token Protiva everywhere it is used, so one find-and-replace substitutes the final
name. Store-level numbers refer to FreshMart Express, Koramangala (store ID BLR-KOR-014) unless
stated otherwise. Period for all 30-day figures is 1 September 2026 to 30 September 2026.

Real brands named in this document (Hershey's, Sofit) appear only as competitors in the chilled
protein and dairy drinks bay, because they are visible in the shelf footage used by the planogram
compliance feature.

---

## 1. Name candidates

| Candidate | Rationale |
| --- | --- |
| Protiva | Reads as protein plus viva, pronounceable in English, Hindi and Kannada, and works as a single word on a 45g bar wrapper and a 200ml bottle neck label without abbreviation. |
| Pushti | Means nourishment in Sanskrit and Hindi, which gives everyday credibility rather than gym credibility, but it skews traditional and reads older than the target shopper. |

**Pick: Protiva.** It carries the protein claim in the name itself, which matters when the brand has
to compete for a glance in a chilled bay against established dairy brands, and it stretches across
bars, shakes and salads without sounding like a supplement.

Substitute token: Protiva

---

## 2. Positioning

**Positioning statement:** Protiva is everyday protein in formats a working person already buys,
a bar, a bottle and a bowl, with the protein number printed on the front and a price that sits inside
a normal grocery basket.

**Shopper:** Urban Bengaluru, 24 to 38, salaried, lives within 2 km of a modern-trade grocery, buys
twice a week, skips or shortens one meal a day on weekdays, tracks protein loosely but does not use
supplements. Average basket at this store is Rs 740.
