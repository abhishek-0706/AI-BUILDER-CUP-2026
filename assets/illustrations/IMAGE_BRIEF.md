# Role character images — brief for ChatGPT

Three images for the role cards on the RetailIQ start screen. Each card already has a slot that
shows the image as soon as the file exists, so nothing else needs building.

## What to deliver

| File name (exact) | Who | Card |
|---|---|---|
| `field-sales-rep.png` | Field sales rep in a store | "I am visiting stores" |
| `sales-manager.png` | Sales manager with their team | "I lead a sales team" |
| `shopper.png` | Shopper choosing protein snacks | "I want to know the shopper" |

- **Format:** PNG, **transparent background**
- **Shape:** portrait, 1024 x 1536 (2:3)
- **Framing:** one person, knees up, filling the height of the image, small margin above the head
- **Facing:** body turned slightly to the left (the product images sit on the left of the card)
- **Save to:** this folder, `assets/illustrations/`

They display about 140 px tall on the card, so keep the silhouette clear and simple. Fine detail
will not be seen.

## How to run it in ChatGPT

1. Paste **Step 1** and generate the field sales rep. Regenerate until you like it.
2. In the **same chat**, paste **Step 2** for the manager and then **Step 3** for the shopper.
   Staying in one chat is what keeps the three looking like one family.
3. If a result has a white or grey background, reply: *"Same image, transparent background, PNG."*
4. Download each one and rename it to the exact file name above.

### Step 1 — Field sales rep (also sets the style)

```
Create a premium semi-3D editorial illustration for an enterprise AI product. This image sets the
style for two more characters, so keep it consistent and reusable.

Character: a young Indian FMCG field sales representative, smart casual deep teal polo shirt,
holding a tablet in one hand, a small dark green sales bag on the shoulder, standing beside a
small neat stack of unbranded protein snack bars and drink bottles. Confident, approachable,
natural expression.

Style: realistic human proportions, soft studio lighting from the upper left, soft shadows,
clean rounded forms, subtle facial features, high-quality materials. Sophisticated, not playful.
Colours: deep teal #0C6B5F, dark green #0A3A33, small lime #C6F04A accents, neutral cream and white.

Framing: portrait 2:3, single person, knees up, filling the image height with a small margin above
the head, body turned slightly to the left.

Background: fully transparent PNG. No floor, no room, no scenery.

Do not include: any text, letters, numbers, logos or brand marks on clothing, packs or the tablet
screen. No cartoon, anime, flat vector or stock-photo look.
```

## Check before you save

- [ ] All three look like one family: same face style, lighting and level of detail
- [ ] Background is really transparent (shows a checkerboard in an image viewer), not white
- [ ] No text, letters or logos anywhere, including on packs and the tablet screen
- [ ] The person fills the image height; there isn't a lot of empty space above or below
- [ ] Hands, fingers and the tablet look right (the most common AI mistake)

If a background comes out white and ChatGPT will not make it transparent, save it anyway. The white
can be removed afterwards.
